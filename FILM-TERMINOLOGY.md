# Film terminology review

A dedicated research agent reviewed familiar production language for the 0.5 update.

Use **Low-budget film / Mid-budget film / Big-budget film**, with actual production costs explained underneath. These are relative game categories, not official union budget classifications. Avoid using independent/indie to mean inexpensive, or blockbuster to promise a successful release. The game's displayed reference amounts describe a Standard production plan; Shoestring plans cost less. Talent, directing and marketing are additional.

SAG-AFTRA uses Low Budget terminology for specific production agreements: https://www.sagaftra.org/low-budget . SAGindie explains the agreement categories: https://www.sagindie.org/signatory/ .

Keep familiar task descriptions such as casting, cast & director, production budget, shooting schedule, start filming and filming wrapped. ScreenSkills describes these producer responsibilities in its [producer handbook](https://www.screenskills.com/media/3525/2020-04-13-producers-handbook.pdf) and [industry glossary](https://www.screenskills.com/online-learning/glossary).

The agent also suggested Shoestring / Basic / Standard / High-end / Top-of-the-line as possible spending-tier labels. These are interface suggestions rather than official industry categories. This update retains existing slider tiers while clarifying their consequences.
