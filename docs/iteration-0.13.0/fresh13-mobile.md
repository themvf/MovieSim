# Fresh 13 mobile playtest

Fresh isolated Edge browser, 390×844 mobile viewport. Used rendered UI text, controls and screenshots only; no source, save, or engine access. Completed a film through theatrical release and first streaming royalty. No game edits.

## Actual play

Initial session: selected Last Call ($70,000 advertised), acquired it (cash fell $72,000), auditioned Arlo Bennett (80–100), compared weak supporting auditions with Kai Sato (80–90), hired Kai and emerging director Alma Moss. Chose local exteriors/practical effects and greenlit. Browser-tool timeout lost this nonpersistent session during week advancement; this is an automation limitation, not a game bug.

Restarted in an isolated persistent profile. New randomized catalog. Acquired Lights Off, hired Arlo Rivera after 75–85 audition, Sasha Chen after 80–90 audition, and Jude Moreau (70–80 expected direction). Selected mostly interiors and imply-rather-than-show effects, standard departmental budgets, recommended eight-week schedule. Planned total $1,065,500. At week 3 chose crowd-pleasing edit over challenging/balanced alternatives. Wrapped week 9, bought $45,000 test screening (95/100), chose week 12 because it had only one nearby release, spent $65,000 on social marketing, and signed Meridian partner distribution (40% share; $390,000 recovery).

Opened week 12 at displayed $1.5m gross: critics 65, fans 75, lead 60, supporting 90, direction 70. Opened the Leon Okafor half-fee opportunity; its modal said to acquire/develop a screenplay but supplied no action beyond closing. Continued theatrical run to week 20: $5.5m gross, $730,244 approximate profit before streaming advance. Selected PictureHouse weekly royalties instead of BingeBox upfront license. After signing, project report showed $780,244 profit, including $50,000 streaming advance. Advanced to week 21: streaming earned $61,137, 51 weeks left, studio cash $6,691,381. Complete practical lifecycle achieved.

## Experience

Enjoyment 7/10; clarity 7/10. The role auditions and low-cost newcomer discoveries were strongest. Choosing a quiet release week and a royalty contract felt consequential. Production choices fit the horror premise, and budget feedback made greenlight comprehensible. The automatic next-decision routing kept the lifecycle moving. Release learning report was useful, especially separating studio receipts from gross.

Mobile layout stayed within 390px (document scrollWidth 390). Large single-column cards and dialogs were readable; no blocking mobile layout bug encountered. Long casting cards and project reports demand substantial scrolling. Full-page screenshots show long-page context, so fixed/dialog positioning in those images is not itself a viewport bug.

## Findings

1. **Displayed forecast contradicts displayed outcome (medium).** Opening report says expected $1,500,000–$2,500,000, result $1,500,000, and marks it Below / Below your forecast. The underlying amount may be lower before rounding, but the player sees an in-range result called below. Screenshot: fresh13-mobile-release.png.
2. **Acquisition price discrepancy (needs confirmation).** First session Last Call advertised and confirmed $70,000, but immediately showed $72,000 spent and $5,928,000 cash. No explanation was visible. Could be rounding or rights overhead; player cannot reconcile purchase from the confirmation. Initial session was lost before further examination.
3. **Opportunity dead end (minor UX).** Find a role on Leon Okafor opens a modal telling an empty-development-slate player to acquire/develop a screenplay, with no direct button to do so. Discount is appealing but the next step is unnecessarily manual.
4. **Test-screening feedback is thin.** Paying $45,000 returned only 95/100 plus caveat. Final fans 75 is allowed by estimates but the large discrepancy had no specific interpretation; it weakened the value of that purchase for me.

## Top three improvements

1. Align forecast labels and displayed monetary precision; include exact acquisition subtotal/fees so visible arithmetic reconciles.
2. Give talent opportunities a direct Find screenplay/Create original action and retain the actor/discount context while browsing suitable projects.
3. Turn test screening into two or three actionable audience notes linked to release/marketing or edit choices, rather than a lone score; compact mobile casting comparison would also reduce scrolling.
